# Homepage (index.html)

## Scope and mode
Full redesign of the single-page portfolio (index.html, assets/css/style.css, assets/js/main.js). Static files on GitHub Pages. Mode: Persuade. Code-led build (no image generation).

## Audience, job, action
Recruiters and hiring managers deciding in seconds whether to shortlist; secondary: teams seeking accessibility audits or consulting. They must leave knowing who she is (Shivani), her two equal halves (QA leadership and accessibility), her uncopiable proof, and how to reach her (LinkedIn, email; GitHub secondary).

## Confirmed decisions
- Structure may change freely; every existing fact survives somewhere.
- Headshot prominent in the first viewport.
- Copy allowed to change: surname in the headline, a verified accessibility statement, contact copy inviting full-time roles and accessibility audit or consulting work.
- Project headings are the project names (Follett Destiny Discover, Axis 360, Samsung DeX); the role sits in the line below.
- Accessibility statement: states the WCAG 2.2 AA target and keyboard/reduced-motion support (verified at build). Screen-reader testing will be done by the user or Shivani before launch; the build carries a clearly marked placeholder line for which screen readers, to be filled once confirmed. No SR claims until then.
- Anti-goals: cold or bureaucratic tone, developer cosplay (terminal/monospace), gimmicky pass/fail stamps or scores, annotation overload, airport cosplay (planes, gate numbers, flip-board departures), kickers above headings, tag clouds, infinite motion, hover-lift on non-links, translucent nav.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Her career signposted like an international transit system: readable in seconds, under pressure, by anyone, which is her own discipline. Refuses the category default of pill-eyebrow hero, headshot with orbit, and résumé cards with tag clouds.

OWN-WORLD: Light ground; black sign bands and panels carry the high-contrast load; wayfinding yellow is the one owned hue at full strength, used only on signs, arrows, and markers, never on reading text. A true signage typeface with a point of view (not Inter), set large with generous letterspacing on sign lines; tabular figures for every date and number. Components: sign bands, directory rows with arrows, route line with stops, credential plaques, arrow-sign buttons. Every claim seamed to its provenance (employer, dates, issuer). One highlighted proof line per section.

STORY: The visitor arrives at the sign and knows who and what in one glance, sees the proof directory, follows the fork to either half (Accessibility or QA leadership) with its evidence, traces the career route, checks the credentials, reads how the site itself was built, and exits to LinkedIn or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Directory nav across the top as destinations with arrows and a You-are-here marker. Below, the arrival sign: "Shivani" at poster scale flush-left on a black sign band, title as the second sign line; the headshot large as a lit panel beside it, equal weight to the name. Under the name, one directory row of proof with sources: 14 years · Samsung R&D Institute India, 2008–2020 · Photon, 2021–2024 · DHS Trusted Tester (Section 508). Primary actions LinkedIn and Email as yellow arrow signs, left, above the fold. Mobile stacks name, photo, proof, actions.

FORM: Wayfinding System (international airport and transit signage, universal-design pictograms), position 1 on the ordered list, chosen as the pick card over the assigned Calibration Target. Seed key 038a9c2e. Signature interaction: directory nav with a You-are-here marker following scroll, announced as current location. Motion: arrows nudge once on hover/focus; nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
